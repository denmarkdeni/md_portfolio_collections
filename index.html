About

I spent four years as a backend engineer before I realized I liked explaining the "why" behind the code more than writing it alone. Now I run cohorts that take people from print("hello world") to shipping fullstack apps with auth, databases, and deployment.

How I teach

Every concept is tied to a project. You don't watch me code — you build alongside me, break things, and fix them.

Who I teach

Career switchers, CS grads who never shipped anything, and teams upskilling into Python fullstack roles.

Where I've trained

In-house bootcamps, corporate upskilling programs, and one-on-one mentorships across three time zones.
